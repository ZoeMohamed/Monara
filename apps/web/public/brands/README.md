# Bank and merchant artwork

Retrieved on 15 October 2026 from the official sites or their explicitly linked asset CDNs. These files preserve the published bytes, colours, proportions and clear space; only filenames change. Display with `object-fit: contain`. Do not redraw, recolour, stretch or imply endorsement. The marks remain the property of their respective owners; the linked source pages retain their usage guidance.

Artwork is bundled locally so showing a financial record does not request an image from a merchant or third-party logo service. Unknown merchants/accounts keep the generic fallback. Logo selection is presentation only and must never invent an account mapping.
